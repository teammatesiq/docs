# Flow Path asset specification

## Approved structure

The mark combines two forward-moving ribbons with the TeamMatesIQ corporate wordmark. TeamMates is an endorsed product wordmark; Admin TeamMate and future role names remain product titles and must not become separate logos. The brand promise is never part of the logo or primary lockup.

## Construction

- Symbol source grid: 160 × 85 units
- Ribbon depth: 22 units
- Ribbon separation: 8 units at the left edge
- Flat terminal cuts preserve clarity at small sizes
- Colour symbol: upper ribbon Clear Blue; lower ribbon Aqua
- Wordmark: Manrope Bold converted to vector outlines

## Clear space

Maintain clear space on every side equal to one ribbon depth, measured from the visible edge of the artwork. No text, imagery, border or interface control may enter this area.

## Minimum sizes

- Corporate horizontal lockup: 160 px digital / 32 mm print
- Endorsed TeamMates lockup: 190 px digital / 38 mm print
- Standalone symbol: 16 px digital / 4 mm print
- At sizes below the horizontal-lockup minimum, use the standalone symbol

## Backgrounds

- Use the primary lockup on Soft White, white or other pale neutral backgrounds.
- Use the reversed lockup on Deep Navy or sufficiently dark photography.
- Use single-colour navy where production constraints prevent the two-colour mark.
- Use single-colour white on dark one-colour applications.
- Do not place Aqua text on a light background.

## Prohibited treatments

- Do not add gradients, shadows, glow or 3D effects.
- Do not recolour individual ribbons outside the approved variants.
- Do not stretch, rotate, crop or rearrange the mark.
- Do not add the brand promise to a logo lockup.
- Do not create separate logos for Admin TeamMate or Project TeamMate.

## File naming

Names use lowercase kebab case and identify brand, lockup and treatment. SVG is the master format; PNG exports are convenience derivatives.
