# 1. Purpose

This standard governs customer-facing naming, terminology, tone and editorial usage across website, product, sales, onboarding, support and campaign content.

# 2. Naming Rules

| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| TeamMatesIQ | Company and master brand. Preserve this capitalisation. |
| TeamMates | Customer-facing product. |
| Admin TeamMate | SME v1 role. Preserve the capital M in TeamMate. |
| Project TeamMate | Approved future role name; exclude from SME v1 marketing. |
| TeamMate | Capitalise when referring to a defined product role. |
| teammate | Lowercase only when used generically. |
| TMOS | Internal platform terminology for SME v1. |

# 3. Preferred Terminology

| Use | Avoid | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| digital teammate | digital employee, digital colleague, robot, bot | Matches the product identity without implying employment or humanity. |
| hire an Admin TeamMate | buy, deploy or install an AI | Approved acquisition metaphor. |
| your Admin TeamMate | AI assistant, virtual worker | Makes the role understandable without leading with technology. |
| work | jobs, autonomous operations | Plain and accurate. |
| review and approve | human in the loop | Customer language rather than technical jargon. |
| permissions | access privileges | Familiar customer language. |
| Paused | Suspended, when customer-initiated | Paused is customer-facing language for `suspended/customer_paused`. |
| Probation | training mode, testing mode | Canonical lifecycle state. |
| learns how your business works | trains itself | Avoids uncontrolled-learning implications. |
| informed and in control | fully autonomous | Accurately represents human accountability. |
| powered by AI, where disclosure is relevant | AI-powered as the lead proposition | AI is transparent but not the primary benefit. |

# 4. Lifecycle Language

The canonical lifecycle is:

**Configuring → Probation → Active → Suspended → Archived**

- Use **Paused** only for a customer-initiated pause, mapped to `status = suspended` and `suspension_reason = customer_paused`.
- Use **Suspended** for system, administrative, contractual or security contexts where that status is accurate.
- Explain Probation as a controlled settling-in period. Do not imply that elapsed time alone guarantees completion.
- Do not invent additional customer-facing lifecycle states.

# 5. Hiring Metaphor Rules

Approved uses include:

- Hire an Admin TeamMate.
- Meet your Admin TeamMate.
- Your Admin TeamMate starts in Probation.

The hiring metaphor must not imply:

- a contract of employment
- employee rights or status
- human identity or consciousness
- a guaranteed replacement for an employee

Terms, disclosures and legal copy must describe the actual digital service relationship accurately.

# 6. Tone Rules

## 6.1 Capable

State what the product does in active, specific language. Avoid hype, superlatives and unsupported certainty.

## 6.2 Human

Use warm, natural language. Do not pretend the TeamMate has emotions, a personal life or a physical presence.

## 6.3 Reassuring

Explain controls and limitations plainly. Do not make absolute safety or security promises.

## 6.4 Practical

Refer to recognisable work and outcomes. Avoid abstract transformation language.

## 6.5 Straightforward

Prefer short sentences, plain English and a clear next action. Explain unavoidable technical terms.

## 6.6 Progressive

Sound modern and intelligent without futuristic, science-fiction or novelty-AI language.

# 7. Examples

Use:

> Your Admin TeamMate keeps routine admin moving—drafting, organising and following up while you stay informed and in control.

Avoid:

> Our revolutionary AI-powered automation platform transforms operational productivity.

Use:

> Designed to keep you informed and in control.

Avoid:

> Completely safe and secure.

Use:

> Works within the permissions and controls you set.

Avoid:

> Works autonomously without supervision.

# 8. Editorial Conventions

- Use UK English.
- Use sentence case for headings, labels and buttons unless a governed component specifies otherwise.
- Address the customer as **you** and the organisation as **your business** where natural.
- Prefer verbs that describe work: prepare, draft, organise, review, approve, pause.
- Use contractions where they improve naturalness, but keep legal, security and error content precise.
- Avoid unexplained acronyms, internal identifiers and implementation terminology.

# 9. Review Checklist

Before publication, confirm that content:

1. Uses the approved brand and role names.
2. Represents released capability accurately.
3. Uses lifecycle terms correctly.
4. Keeps AI transparent but secondary to customer value.
5. Preserves human control and accountability.
6. Contains no prohibited or unsupported claim.
7. Meets accessibility and plain-language expectations.
8. Has the required Product, Legal, Security or Brand review.
