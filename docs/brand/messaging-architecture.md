# 1. Purpose

This document converts the approved positioning into a consistent message hierarchy for TeamMatesIQ, TeamMates and Admin TeamMate.

# 2. Message Hierarchy

| Level | Audience job | Approved message |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate promise | Understand why TeamMatesIQ exists | **Helping businesses work smarter.** |
| Product promise | Understand the main value of TeamMates | **Create more capacity for the work that matters.** |
| Role proposition | Understand what Admin TeamMate does | **Hire an Admin TeamMate that keeps on top of your email, meetings, actions and routine admin.** |
| Primary action | Know what to do next | **Hire an Admin TeamMate.** |
| Trust reassurance | Understand how control begins | **It starts in Probation, so you can review and approve its work while it learns how your business operates.** |

# 3. Audience Message Priorities

## 3.1 Business owners and Managing Directors

Lead with regained capacity, reduced administrative burden and confidence that people remain in control.

## 3.2 Operations leaders

Lead with reliable follow-through, visibility of work, permissions and repeatable operating discipline.

## 3.3 Office managers

Lead with help keeping email, meetings, actions and routine administration moving without creating more coordination work.

# 4. Value-Pillar Messages

## 4.1 Capacity

**Message:** Create more capacity for the work that matters.

**Proof direction:** Show specific administrative work completed or prepared. Do not publish numerical time-saving claims without approved evidence.

## 4.2 Keeps work moving

**Message:** Your Admin TeamMate helps keep routine administration moving.

**Proof direction:** Demonstrate released support for email, meetings, actions and routine documents.

## 4.3 Informed and in control

**Message:** You define permissions and remain informed and in control.

**Proof direction:** Show review, approval, evidence, audit and pause controls accurately.

## 4.4 A controlled start

**Message:** Your Admin TeamMate begins in Probation while learning how your business operates.

**Proof direction:** Explain that external actions require approval in SME v1 and that additional autonomy is not granted automatically.

# 5. Recommended Core Copy

## 5.1 Short description

TeamMates helps SMEs hire capable digital teammates that get essential work done while people remain informed, in control and accountable.

## 5.2 Admin TeamMate description

Your Admin TeamMate keeps routine admin moving—drafting, organising and following up while you stay informed and in control.

## 5.3 AI explanation

TeamMates uses AI to help your Admin TeamMate understand information, prepare work and support routine administration within the permissions and controls you set.

## 5.4 Probation explanation

Your Admin TeamMate starts with a controlled settling-in period, learning how your business works while you review and approve its work.

# 6. Message Order by Touchpoint

| Touchpoint | Lead | Support | Reassure | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage | Capacity | Practical Admin TeamMate work | Informed and in control | Hire an Admin TeamMate |
| Product page | Role proposition | Released capabilities | Permissions and Probation | Hire an Admin TeamMate |
| Onboarding | Next useful step | What the TeamMate needs | Why permissions and approvals matter | Continue setup |
| Product interface | Current work or decision | Evidence and status | Limitations or uncertainty | Review, approve or provide input |
| Sales material | Business problem and capacity | Practical use cases | Claims evidence and controls | Start the hiring journey |
| Support | Customer outcome | Clear resolution steps | What remains safe or unaffected | Complete the next action |

# 7. Message Boundaries

- Do not lead with AI.
- Do not imply human identity, consciousness or employment status.
- Do not describe planned capabilities as available.
- Do not imply unrestricted autonomy.
- Do not use numerical, comparative, security or performance claims without the evidence and approval required by the Claims Register.
- Keep Project TeamMate outside SME v1 acquisition material.
- Keep TMOS internal unless a customer-facing platform reference is specifically required.
