# 1. Purpose

This document defines the approved brand foundation for TeamMatesIQ and the TeamMates product. It governs positioning, audience, promise, personality, brand architecture and the core creative direction for SME v1.

It does not define product behaviour, technical architecture, security controls, legal requirements, pricing or release gates. Customer-facing claims must remain consistent with released capability and the [Claims Register](claims-register.md).

# 2. Brand Architecture

| Level | Approved name | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate and master brand | TeamMatesIQ | Owns the company identity and wider portfolio. |
| Product | TeamMates | The customer-facing product through which organisations hire and manage digital teammates. |
| SME v1 role | Admin TeamMate | The first commercial TeamMate. |
| Future role | Project TeamMate | Approved future role name; outside SME v1. |
| Internal platform | TMOS | Internal platform name for SME v1 unless a customer-facing reference is specifically required. |

Recommended endorsement where context requires it: **TeamMates, by TeamMatesIQ**.

Individual TeamMate roles are product titles within one shared identity. They must not be treated as separate brands or given unrelated logos.

# 3. Target Audience

## 3.1 Primary buyer

Business owners and Managing Directors of UK service-led SMEs with approximately 5–50 employees.

The defining need is that senior people and their teams still spend too much time on routine administration.

## 3.2 Secondary audiences

- Operations leaders
- Office managers

## 3.3 Customer problem

Routine email, meeting, action and administrative work consumes leadership attention, team capacity and cognitive effort.

# 4. Positioning

TeamMates is a product through which SMEs hire capable digital teammates to get essential work done while people retain visibility, control and accountability.

TeamMates must not be positioned primarily as generic AI software, a chatbot, an agent platform, automation software or a replacement for employees.

# 5. Approved Promise Hierarchy

- **TeamMatesIQ brand promise:** Helping businesses work smarter.
- **TeamMates product promise:** Create more capacity for the work that matters.
- **Admin TeamMate proposition:** Hire an Admin TeamMate that keeps on top of your email, meetings, actions and routine admin.
- **Primary call to action:** Hire an Admin TeamMate.
- **Probation reassurance:** It starts in Probation, so you can review and approve its work while it learns how your business operates.

# 6. Customer Value Pillars

## 6.1 Capacity

Reduce routine administrative work so owners and teams can focus on work that matters.

## 6.2 Useful work

Show practical outcomes across email, meetings, actions and routine administration rather than leading with technology.

## 6.3 Control

Make permissions, approvals, evidence, uncertainty and the ability to pause clear.

## 6.4 Confidence

Build trust through a controlled Probation period, transparent work and claims that do not exceed evidence.

# 7. Brand Personality

| Attribute | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Capable | Confident, useful and focused on outcomes. |
| Human | Warm and natural without pretending a TeamMate is a person. |
| Reassuring | Clear about control, permissions, safeguards and limitations. |
| Practical | Grounded in recognisable business tasks. |
| Straightforward | Concise, accessible and free from unnecessary AI jargon. |
| Progressive | Modern and intelligent without appearing futuristic. |

# 8. Tone

TeamMatesIQ should sound:

- confident, never boastful
- friendly, never overly casual
- intelligent, never technical for its own sake
- reassuring, never defensive
- commercially credible, never corporate or vague

# 9. Core Creative Direction: The Flow

The approved creative territory is **The Flow**.

- **Central idea:** Work flows; capacity opens up.
- **Signature device:** A flowing ribbon or pathway connecting tasks, people and outcomes.
- **Overall feel:** Modern, human, capable, calm and commercially credible.
- **People:** Authentic SME owners and teams in recognisable working environments.
- **Product:** Clear interface imagery showing useful work, review and control.
- **Technology:** Present but understated; never the visual headline.

Robots, androids, artificial people, glowing brains, circuit-board imagery and science-fiction AI clichés are prohibited.

# 10. Brand Governance

The Head of Brand owns this foundation and its supporting standards. Material changes to positioning, architecture, promise, naming or identity require approval by the Programme Director / Orchestrator and must be recorded in the [Brand Decision Log](brand-decision-log.md).

Product, UX, Commercial, Customer Success, Legal, Security, AI Governance and Engineering remain accountable for decisions within their own remits.
