# 1. Purpose

This register controls customer-facing brand, capability, performance, trust and comparative claims. It prevents marketing language from exceeding released capability or available evidence.

The register does not approve product scope. Capability owners must confirm release status before any capability claim is published.

# 2. Claims Classification

| Classification | Publication rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Approved proposition | May be used consistently within this standard. | Create more capacity for the work that matters. |
| Capability claim | May be used only when Product confirms the feature is released for the relevant customer and context. | Your Admin TeamMate drafts meeting follow-ups. |
| Evidence-backed claim | Requires approved, reproducible evidence and identified scope. | Customers save an average of X hours each month. |
| Beta hypothesis | Internal until validated and approved. | Ten hours saved per customer per month. |
| Prohibited claim | Must not be used. | Fully autonomous, error-free, replaces an employee. |

# 3. Approved Proposition Claims

| ID | Claim | Permitted use | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| CLM-001 | Helping businesses work smarter. | TeamMatesIQ corporate brand. | Do not present as a measured performance claim. |
| CLM-002 | Create more capacity for the work that matters. | TeamMates product. | Express as a brand promise; do not attach unverified numerical savings. |
| CLM-003 | Hire an Admin TeamMate that keeps on top of your email, meetings, actions and routine admin. | Admin TeamMate proposition. | Underlying capabilities must be available in the promoted release. |
| CLM-004 | Designed to keep you informed and in control. | Trust reassurance. | Do not convert to an absolute safety or control guarantee. |

# 4. Conditional Capability Claims

These claims are permitted only when Product confirms that the relevant capability is released and the wording matches actual behaviour.

| ID | Claim area | Permitted direction | Required confirmation |
|---|---|---|---|
| CLM-101 | Routine administration | Your Admin TeamMate helps keep routine administration moving. | Relevant Admin TeamMate workflows are released. |
| CLM-102 | Email, meetings and actions | It can support email, meetings, actions and routine administrative work. | Each named capability is available in the promoted package. |
| CLM-103 | Permissions | You define its permissions and remain informed and in control. | UX and security controls accurately support the statement. |
| CLM-104 | Approvals | Work requiring approval is presented for review before it proceeds. | Approval boundaries and exceptions are accurately disclosed. |
| CLM-105 | Probation | It begins in Probation while learning how your business operates. | Onboarding and Probation behaviour is implemented as specified. |
| CLM-106 | Pause | You can pause your Admin TeamMate. | Paused correctly maps to `suspended/customer_paused`. |
| CLM-107 | AI | TeamMates uses AI to help prepare work within the permissions and controls you set. | Legal, privacy and AI disclosures remain complete and accurate. |

# 5. Evidence Required Before Publication

The following need a named owner, defined cohort, measurement method, period, sample size, exclusions, source record, review date and approval:

- hours or money saved
- productivity percentage improvements
- draft acceptance, quality or accuracy rates
- response or completion-time improvements
- customer-satisfaction results
- security, reliability or performance comparisons
- market-leading, best, first or unique claims
- any claim that a TeamMate performs like or replaces a human employee

# 6. Beta Hypotheses

| ID | Internal hypothesis | Current status | External use |
|---|---|---|---|
| HYP-001 | At least 10 hours of administrative effort saved per customer per month once established. | Unvalidated product hypothesis. | Prohibited until evidence-backed and approved. |
| HYP-002 | At least 70% of routine prepared drafts accepted without significant rewrite during mature beta usage. | Unvalidated product hypothesis. | Prohibited until evidence-backed and approved. |

# 7. Prohibited Claims

Do not claim or imply that TeamMates or a TeamMate is:

- fully autonomous
- error-free
- completely safe or secure
- guaranteed to save a specific amount of time or money
- a human, employee or conscious entity
- a replacement for an employee
- able to act without relevant permissions, policy or approval
- compliant with a law, regulation or standard without Legal approval and supporting evidence

# 8. Evidence Record Template

Every evidence-backed claim must record:

- claim ID and exact wording
- owner
- audience, channel and geography
- evidence source and repository location
- measurement method and date range
- cohort and sample size
- exclusions and limitations
- Product review
- Legal/Compliance review
- Brand approval
- approval date and expiry or review date

# 9. Governance

- Brand owns the wording and register.
- Product confirms released capability.
- QA validates test and evaluation evidence.
- Data confirms measurement quality and lineage.
- Legal/Compliance reviews regulated, comparative and employment-adjacent claims.
- Security reviews security and control claims.
- Commercial confirms the intended channel and offer.

Any material change to an approved claim must be reviewed as a new claim.
